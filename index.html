What's New — Recent Updates

This update extends the project with a Buehler-inspired knowledge-graph analysis stage (Phase 3), along with a thorough dataset cleanup and quality-control pass on the original 942-row dataset.

  • Dataset cleanup: case/whitespace normalization across categorical fields (e.g. Modified variants merged); semantic merges (PA6 ↔ Nylon 6, PA66 ↔ Nylon66, Three-Point Bend ↔ Flexural Test (3PB)); missing-data markers (?) converted to NaN so improvements with negatives can be analyzed; a unit-error correction on row 183 (Epoxy matrix strength 0.12 MPa → 12 MPa).
  • arcsinh transformation: six new arcsinh columns added next to existing Log10 columns. Unlike log10, arcsinh accepts negative values, so improvement columns (where log10 produced many NaN) become usable — e.g. the strain-improvement column went from 91 to 440 usable values (4.8× recovery).
  • Phase 3 — Buehler-style knowledge graph: deterministic concept-graph construction from the categorical fields, transformer-based node embeddings (all-MiniLM-L6-v2, 384-d), cosine-similarity semantic graph, and a complete graph that places every experiment and every numerical regime as a node alongside the 245 categorical concepts.
  • Transparent reporting in Phase 2 & UBMK 2026 scripts: filtering logic now distinguishes "dataset total / clusterable / edge-eligible" honestly (e.g. UBMK 2026 reports 942 → 746 → 727 instead of a single misleading number).
  • Chapter 7 — Generative reasoning (Buehler §2.3 implementation): five LLM-derived polymer/clay design hypotheses (H1–H5) generated from sub-graph queries of the complete knowledge graph. Two hypotheses (H2 thermoset over-loading; H5 CNBR + unmodified MMT) survive a skeptical cross-framing and are accompanied by explicit falsification criteria and a 20-specimen experimental validation protocol.

PHASE 3 Buehler-Inspired Knowledge Graph Analysis

Adaptation of Buehler (2024) §2.1–2.2 methodology to the tabular polymer-MMT dataset. A deterministic concept graph is built from categorical fields, enriched with arcsinh-space numerical statistics on every node and edge. Concept descriptions are then encoded by a sentence-transformer (all-MiniLM-L6-v2, 384-d) and compared via cosine similarity to reveal semantic structure that the co-occurrence graph alone cannot.

CHAPTER 7 Generative Reasoning over the Knowledge Graph

Implementation of Buehler (2024) §2.3 — graph-driven hypothesis generation — that the base Phase 3 pipeline had left as future work. Five sub-graphs are extracted from the complete graph, each isolating a thematically distinct region of the polymer/clay design space. An LLM then reasons over each sub-graph and proposes a polymer/clay composite design suggested by — but not yet realised in — the data. Five hypotheses (H1–H5) are produced; two of them (H2 thermoset over-loading mechanism; H5 CNBR + unmodified MMT) survive an independent skeptical re-framing and are accompanied by a concrete experimental falsification protocol.

H1 — Hybrid-Filler Epoxy
MMT + GO/silica at 5–8 wt% in high-modulus epoxy. Partial survival.
H2 — Thermoset Over-Loading ⭐
Failure mode at >3 wt% Modified MMT with intercalated dispersion. Strong survival.
H3 — PA6/PP + Elastomer Compatibilizer
SEBS-g-MA at 5–10 wt% to recover lost ductility. Partial survival.
H4 — Low-MW PA6 at High Loading
PA6 + 10–15 wt% MMT pushes ΔE > 200 %. Weak support.
H5 — CNBR + Unmodified MMT ⭐
Cost-driven, polar-affinity exfoliation at 10–20 wt%. Strongest hypothesis.
