Metric definitions
------------------
silhouette    : silhouette coefficient on polymer nodes vs. ground-truth families (higher = better separation; range -1..+1)
ARI           : Adjusted Rand Index between K-means clustering and true family labels (1 = perfect, 0 = random)
NMI           : Normalized Mutual Information between K-means clustering and true labels (1 = identical)
cohes_intra   : mean cosine similarity for polymer pairs WITHIN the same family
cohes_inter   : mean cosine similarity for polymer pairs ACROSS families
cohes_gap     : cohes_intra - cohes_inter (positive = embeddings discriminate families)
graph_spearman: Spearman correlation between (1 - cosine) and graph shortest-path length
graph_p_value : p-value of the Spearman correlation
graph_n_pairs : number of sampled concept pairs used in the correlation
